INSTALL ON LINUX (64-bit)

Plugin (VST3) - for Bitwig, REAPER, Ardour and other Linux DAWs
1. Unzip the download.
2. Copy the .vst3 folder into:  ~/.vst3/
   (create the folder if it doesn't exist:  mkdir -p ~/.vst3)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.

Standalone app (no DAW needed)
1. Open a terminal in the unzipped folder.
2. Run:  chmod +x "./MIDI GEN"   then   "./MIDI GEN"
3. Pick your audio and MIDI devices under Options > Audio/MIDI Settings.

Needs: ALSA or JACK audio (standard on most distros).
