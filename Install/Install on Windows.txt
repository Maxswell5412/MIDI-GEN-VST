MIDI GEN by MB Audio - install on Windows
==========================================
Works on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. Format: VST3 (Ableton, FL Studio, Reaper,
Bitwig, Studio One, Cubase...).

1. Right-click MIDIGEN-Windows.zip and choose "Extract All...".

2. Open File Explorer and paste this into the address bar, then press Enter:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Create the VST3 folder if it isn't there.)

3. Drag the whole MIDI GEN.vst3 folder into it. Click "Continue" if Windows
   asks for administrator permission.

4. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.
   Ableton: Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins > turn on
   "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then click Rescan.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
   MIDI GEN appears under MB Audio as an instrument.

QUICK START
- Put a chord clip on a MIDI track (for example one dragged out of HOOKSMITH)
  and put MIDI GEN on the same track.
- Press LISTEN, then play from the start of the clip. MIDI GEN learns it
  (pick 4, 8 or 16 bars to match your clip).
- Pick a STYLE and hit GENERATE: 8 new progressions in the same key and rhythm.
- Click a card to hear it instead of your clip. Drag a card into your DAW.
- SIMILARITY = how close to your chords, COLOR = how rich, SPICE = passing
  chords and twists, BASS MOTION = inversions and pedal bass.
- LOAD puts your own synth on it, or use the built-in keys.

If Windows Defender / SmartScreen warns about it, that's because it isn't
code-signed yet. Choose "More info" > "Run anyway" or allow it.
