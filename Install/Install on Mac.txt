MIDI GEN by MB Audio - install on a Mac
========================================
Works on Intel and Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) Macs, macOS 11 or newer.
Formats: AU (Ableton, Logic, GarageBand...) and VST3 (Ableton, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig...).

1. Double-click MIDIGEN-Mac.zip to unzip it.

2. In Finder, click Go > Go to Folder... and paste:
      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   Drag MIDI GEN.component into that folder.

3. Go > Go to Folder... again and paste:
      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   Drag MIDI GEN.vst3 into that folder.
   (If a folder doesn't exist yet, create it with File > New Folder.)

4. MIDI GEN isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it until you run this
   once. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this whole
   line and press Return:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"MIDI GEN.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"MIDI GEN.vst3"

5. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.
   Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use Audio Units v2" and
   "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then click Rescan.
   Logic: it scans by itself on launch.
   MIDI GEN appears under MB Audio as an instrument.

QUICK START
- Put a chord clip on a MIDI track (for example one dragged out of HOOKSMITH)
  and put MIDI GEN on the same track.
- Press LISTEN, then play from the start of the clip. MIDI GEN learns it
  (pick 4, 8 or 16 bars to match your clip).
- Pick a STYLE and hit GENERATE: 8 new progressions in the same key and rhythm.
- Click a card to hear it instead of your clip. Drag a card into your DAW.
- SIMILARITY = how close to your chords, COLOR = how rich, SPICE = passing
  chords and twists, BASS MOTION = inversions and pedal bass.
- LOAD puts your own synth on it, or use the built-in keys.
